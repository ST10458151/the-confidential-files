## 📜 Case Briefing

**Johannesburg, South Africa. 11:47 PM.**

During an exclusive evening at the Vale Estate, heiress **Amelia Vale** disappears without explanation. Her phone is discovered beside a shattered champagne glass. Estate security cameras were disabled for exactly eleven minutes.

Four suspects. Conflicting stories. A trail of evidence that doesn't quite add up.

You are **Investigator #0047**. Your mission is to piece together what happened, identify the person responsible and support your accusation with evidence.

> **CASE FILE 001 — CONFIDENTIAL**
>
> *The answer is in the details. Trust nothing until you can prove it.*

All characters and events are fictional.

## 🎮 Play the Mystery

**[Open The Confidential Files ↗](https://st10458151.github.io/the-confidential-files/)**

This is a browser-based single-player game. No download or account is required.

### Your investigation

1. **Open the case file.** Read the briefing to understand the disappearance.
2. **Investigate the suspects.** Review four dossiers with motives, alibis and observations.
3. **Examine the evidence.** Inspect seven clues and uncover deeper forensic notes.
4. **Conduct interrogations.** Reveal eight recorded responses and look for contradictions.
5. **Build the evidence board.** Match clues to suspects to confirm three meaningful connections.
6. **Review your detective notebook.** Keep track of what you've discovered.
7. **File your accusation.** Select a suspect, motive and supporting clue.
8. **Discover your result.** Receive a case outcome based on your conclusions and the evidence you've collected.

The investigation saves progress automatically in your current browser. You can reopen the case for another attempt.

## 🎨 Design & Atmosphere

The Confidential Files has a **dark-academia detective aesthetic**, deliberately distinct from my other portfolio applications.

Its visual identity combines midnight charcoal, deep burgundy, aged parchment, espresso brown and antique gold. The interface features original CSS illustrations, faux classified stamps, suspect dossiers, document-style panels and typewriter-inspired details.

The emphasis is on **immersive storytelling through interface design**, without relying on a large game framework or external image assets.

## 💻 Running Locally

**1. Clone the repository**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ST10458151/the-confidential-files.git
cd the-confidential-files
```

**2. Start a local server**

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

**3. Open your browser**

Visit [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) and begin the investigation.

You can also open `index.html` directly in a modern browser.

## 🔐 Privacy & Project Notes

This is an **original fictional frontend game built as a portfolio project**.

Game progress is stored in the browser and is not synced or backed up. Clearing your browser storage may erase saved progress.

Because the entire experience runs client-side, the mystery's solution exists in the source files. This is a narrative puzzle experience, not a secure competitive game or a production investigation system.
